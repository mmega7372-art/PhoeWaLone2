ရေးချ — ာ

ရေးချ is written on the right side of a consonant. It gives the long “ah” sound.

က + ာ = ကာစ + ာ = စာထ + ာ = ထာ

မောက်ချ sits on the right and makes the same long “ah” sound. Its top is taller because it is used with a special group of consonants.
