Let’s Learn Four More Signs!

Listen, repeat, and learn how these four Burmese signs make new sounds.

🎈
ုူဲ့
🐰
1. Learn
MEET THE SIGNS

Look, listen and repeat

Press each speaker and repeat the sound three times.

💡

These signs join consonants to make different vowel sounds and tones.

၁
ု

တစ်ချောင်းငင်

Written below a consonant to make the short “u” sound.

၂
ူ

နှစ်ချောင်းငင်

Written below a consonant to make the long “oo” sound.

၃
ဲ

နောက်ပစ်

Written after a consonant to make the “ai” sound.

၄
့

အောက်မြစ်

Written below a consonant to change its tone.
